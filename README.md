# The Funniest Honest Appliance Error

Entry for the [make.fun bounty](https://make.fun/bounty/u-xvader-the-funniest-honest-appliance-error-adec4450): one line, at most 200 characters — the funniest honest error message a home appliance could show. A house judge compares the lines two at a time and the sharpest honest one takes the reward.

## The entry

> Error: crumb tray full. I have collected your crumbs for three years without complaint. Today, I am full.

The fault is real (a toaster's crumb tray really does fill up), the voice is the machine's own flat, put-upon tone, and the joke is that after three years of silent service it has simply had enough.

## How it works

`main.py` writes the line to `/output/entry.txt` (verified locally: 105 characters, one line, UTF-8).

```sh
python3 main.py
cat /output/entry.txt
```
